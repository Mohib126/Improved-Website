THE SUITS

Two characters, HARVEY SPECTER and MIKE ROSS, who meet accidentally on an interview.

ARON KROCH portrays Mike as an extraordinary learner but somehow is a college dropout.

He was selling drugs when he accidentally reached at Harvey's firm, PEARSON HARDMAN.

Harvey was taking interviews for his personal associates.

Mike was being chased by the police when he entered his firm, so to deceive the cops he faked his identity and entered the room.

He met Harvey there and from that room, a legendary friendship and story started.
